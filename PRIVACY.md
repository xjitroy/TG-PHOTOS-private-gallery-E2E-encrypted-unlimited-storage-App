# Privacy and data storage

TG Photos runs on Android and uses your Telegram account's Saved Messages to store application-encrypted backup attachments and authenticated encrypted catalogs. Telegram authentication and transport require account/session information under Telegram's own service policies. TG Photos does not operate an additional hosted storage backend. No advertising/analytics SDK is included in the declared app dependencies.

Photos, videos, audio and documents are read only through Android permissions, selected-file grants or explicitly enabled shared-storage access. Automatic backup applies to folders you enable. Names, original dates, media facts and available embedded metadata are preserved inside encrypted metadata/files. Clean/category filters change the view, not backup eligibility or originals.

Normal cloud-only temporary media remains ciphertext on disk with a configurable size ceiling and adaptive expiry. Device originals open directly. Intentional offline downloads and explicit exports/shares are separate. Export/share grants can create readable copies outside the app; choose recipients and destinations carefully. Android/OEM scheduling and free space affect physical cleanup timing.

Private Vault has a separate password/recovery key, nested cloud encryption and ephemeral viewing. Optional biometric access stores a per-use hardware-authentication-bound encrypted key wrap only on this phone. Biometric enrollment changes, reinstall or phone changes require password/private-key recovery. Sensitive file actions retain fresh password/private-key requirements. No fingerprint image is collected or stored by TG Photos.

Vault creation names are encrypted; locked selectors can show generated date/ID labels. Forgotten-password recovery creates an independent vault and cannot decrypt the old one. A Private Vault replacement, after an explicit acknowledgement, schedules the old vault for permanent deletion after at least 24 server-confirmed hours. Photos also offers optional scheduled deletion. Telegram sign-in authorizes this delayed management route without the forgotten password; cancellation must reach Telegram before deletion starts. Android, network and key availability can delay completion. Minimal unedited lifecycle messages contain opaque account/vault/request IDs and states, without names, filenames, passwords or content keys. These management records remain to recover cancellations/completion after reinstall.

**Keep TG Photos attachments, recovery/catalog messages and Saved Messages in Telegram.** Deleting them there can permanently lose data. App-managed deletion targets app-owned Telegram copies; physical retention inside Telegram or copies created elsewhere cannot be guaranteed erased by this app. Keep recovery material privately. Clearing Android app data/uninstalling removes local keys/convenience state and can make unrecoverable data inaccessible.

Source and artifacts here do not include owner media, sessions, signing or user encryption keys. A functioning Telegram client APK necessarily contains its client API identity, which is different from account or encryption secrets. For issues, never upload personal media, session databases, recovery phrases or full sensitive logs.


Optional People inference runs on-device after the automatically scheduled, hash-pinned38.9MB public YuNet/SFace model download (unless People is explicitly off). Photo pixels are not sent to an AI service. Names, small reference crops, embeddings and cloud-file membership are authenticated encrypted local metadata; reference snapshots, model identifiers and confirmed own-face preference can sync as ciphertext to the owner's Telegram Saved Messages for recovery. Review matching errors. Removing a group tombstones active recovery. New complete snapshots are readback-authenticated before older authenticated app-owned snapshots are removed. Private Vault is excluded.

Optional photo OCR downloads28.5MB of hash-pinned public language models. Photos/text are not sent to an OCR provider; derived text/word boxes stay in an authenticated encrypted local index, with pause/clear controls in Search. Printed-text clues can suggest limited topics, not guaranteed scene understanding. Google Lens sends a selected normal photo to another app only after explicit confirmation and follows Google's privacy policy. User copying shares chosen text through the system clipboard; TG Photos' Copy buttons mark it sensitive and expire their own clip after60seconds.

Moving a previously indexed photo into Private Vault removes its local derived text and face references and retires older authenticated app-owned face snapshots before removing the original. An encrypted, independently scoped exclusion ledger prevents those sources/content from being indexed again after recovery. Interrupted cleanup retries and retains originals if incomplete. Other devices while offline, external edited/forwarded copies and Telegram physical retention are outside the erasure guarantee.

Analysis recovery (1.20.40): automatic face/text/scene processing uses on-device specialised models, without an app battery-level or Wi-Fi-only pause. All derived results remain authenticated ciphertext on disk. Ordinary per-photo OCR/scene/face-result checkpoints may also sync as E2E ciphertext to Saved Messages, independently of automatic original backup. Their caption contains a keyed opaque address, not plaintext recognition results or a raw content hash. Conservatively detected cards/documents/dense text and video-preview results stay local; sensitivity detection is not infallible. Unknown sensitivity prevents new reference uploads. Private Vault sources stay excluded, and private-move cleanup includes the new owned result attachments. Locked access/OS scheduling/storage bounds still apply. Existing automatic-backup data/battery policies are unchanged.

The free editor writes explicit selected copies. Edit/Open with another app shares readable content with that user-selected application through a scoped temporary read grant; the app does not grant write access to the original. There is no hosted AI/editor service or paid editor SDK.
